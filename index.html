UniEZ privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

In short

UniEZ keeps your week in one place: classes, training, work and everything else. What you add is saved to your account so it syncs to every phone you sign in on. Only you can see it. UniEZ has no ads, no tracking and doesn't sell or share your data.

What UniEZ stores

Account: your email address and how you sign in (email code or Google). There are no passwords.

Profile and settings: your name, university, main sport, where you row, units, theme, week starts and hourly pay rate.

Calendar: the UCC timetable and Canvas feed links you paste (kept secret: the app can save them but never read them back), the events they contain, events you add, and which ones you tick off or mark cancelled.

Work: rota emails you forward from Bizimply, the shifts read from them, shifts you add, finish times and pay multipliers. The text of forwarded emails is cleared after 30 days, and forwarded emails that aren't rotas are deleted after 90 days.

Training: workouts you log, and workouts imported from Concept2 or Strava if you connect them.

Where it's kept

Your data is stored with Supabase, on servers in Ireland (EU). Forwarded rota emails pass through CloudMailin, which delivers them to UniEZ. Sign-in codes are sent by email through Gmail. If you sign in with Google, Google tells UniEZ your email address and name.

On your phone, UniEZ keeps a copy of your data so it works offline, and keeps your sign-in in the phone's secure storage. Both are removed when you sign out, and neither is included in Android backups.

Who can see it

Only you, when signed in. Every table in the database is locked to your account, so no other UniEZ user can read or change your data. Live sync between your phones sends only "something changed" signals, never your data.

Strava and Concept2

Connecting is optional. UniEZ asks only for permission to read your activities or results, never to change them. The access keys are stored encrypted and the app itself can never read them.

Workouts imported from Strava are shown only to you and are never sent to any AI service or anyone else. Imported workouts are marked "Powered by Strava" with a link back to Strava.

You can disconnect at any time in Settings. For Strava, UniEZ also tells Strava to cancel its access. Workouts already imported stay until you delete them.

AI

UniEZ doesn't use AI today. If AI features are added later, they will be off until you switch them on, Settings will say exactly what is sent, and Strava workouts will never be sent.

Deleting your data

You can delete any event, shift or workout at any time, and an imported workout you delete is never imported again.

Settings → Delete account deletes your account and everything in it straight away: profile, calendar, feeds, shifts, rota emails, workouts and connections. UniEZ also tells Strava to cancel its access. This can't be undone. Supabase keeps short-lived server logs that may include your account's ID; they expire on their own.
